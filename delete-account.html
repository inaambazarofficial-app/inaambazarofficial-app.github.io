Delete Your Inaam Bazar Account

You can delete your account and personal data at any time — inside the app, or by email if you no longer have the app.

App: Inaam Bazar · Developer: Inaam Bazar team

Short summary (Roman Urdu)

  • App mein: Profile → neeche scroll karein → "Account Delete" → "Haan, Delete" → wahi Google account chunein.
  • App nahi hai? Apne Gmail se inaambazarofficial@gmail.com par email karein. Hum 30 din ke andar delete kar denge.
  • Delete ke baad aapke tickets aur progress wapas nahi aa sakte.

Option 1: Delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Inaam Bazar app and log in.
  2. Tap Profile in the bottom menu.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Account Delete".
  4. Read the warning and tap "Haan, Delete".
  5. Choose the same Google account you use for Inaam Bazar to confirm. Your account is deleted right away.

Option 2: Ask us by email

Use this if you have uninstalled the app, cannot log in, or your account is blocked.

  1. Send an email from the Gmail address you used in Inaam Bazar to inaambazarofficial@gmail.com.
  2. Subject: Delete my Inaam Bazar account.
  3. We may reply to confirm it is really you. We will delete your account within 30 days and tell you when it is done.

✉️ Email a delete request

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, ticket balance, streak, badges, stats and referral code.
  • Your private information: email address, phone number (if any) and push notification token.
  • Your login (your Inaam Bazar sign-in is removed).
Deleting is permanent. Your tickets, progress and any unclaimed or pending prize claim are lost and cannot be brought back.

What is kept, and why

A small amount of information is kept so that past draws stay fair and can be checked, and to prevent fraud:

KeptWhy
Past draw entries and ticket history recordsSo past draw results stay correct and can be checked.
Prize claims and winner records (including name and city on the Winners Wall, and approved proof)Proof that prizes were really given; legal and dispute reasons.
Coded (hashed) device ID used for referral checksTo stop the same phone from cheating the referral system again.

If you won a prize and want your proof photo/video removed from the Winners Wall, email us and we will remove it.
