1. Who we are

Inaam Bazar ("we", "us") is a free giveaway and rewards app for users in Pakistan, run by the Inaam Bazar team. This policy explains what information the Inaam Bazar Android app and this website collect, why, and what we do with it.

If you have any question about your data, email us at inaambazarofficial@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

a) From your Google account (when you log in)

  • Your name, email address and Google account ID.
  • A phone number, only if your Google account shares one with apps.

b) Your activity in the app

  • Ticket balance and ticket history (what you earned and spent, and why).
  • Draw entries, game plays and results, daily bonus and streak, missions, badges, quiz answers and poll votes.
  • How many rewarded ads you watched (a count and time, not what the ad showed).
  • Referral information: your referral code, who invited you and who you invited.
  • The avatar picture you choose (from pictures built into the app) and your sound setting. These are saved on your phone.

c) Device information

  • A coded (hashed) form of your phone's Android ID. We use it only to stop referral cheating (the same phone claiming referral rewards again and again). It is created on your phone; the real Android ID is never sent to us.
  • A push notification token, so we can send you draw alerts and winner messages.
  • Basic technical data that Google Firebase collects automatically when the app connects, such as IP address, device type and app version.

d) If you win a prize

  • Claim form: full name, phone number, delivery address, city and province.
  • Winner proof (optional): a photo or video of you with your prize, if you choose to upload one.
  • Cash option: if a prize is paid in cash instead (see our Terms), we will ask for your JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank account details at that time, only to send the payment.
We do not collect your contacts, location (GPS), SMS, call logs, or any payment card details. You never pay anything in Inaam Bazar.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and run your account and keep your tickets and entries safe.
  • To run prize draws fairly and pick winners at random from valid entries.
  • To contact winners, check their claim and deliver their prize.
  • To show the leaderboard, Live Draw and Winners Wall.
  • To send notifications about draws, results and your account. You can turn these off in your phone's settings.
  • To stop cheating, fake accounts and abuse, and to ban accounts that break our rules.
  • To show rewarded ads, which keep the app free.
  • To answer your messages and to follow the law.

4. What other users can see

Some information is shown inside the app to all users. This is part of how a fair, transparent giveaway works:

WhereWhat is shown
Weekly leaderboardYour name, chosen avatar and weekly ticket score.
Live DrawA short form of your name (for example "Ali K.") among the people who entered that draw.
Winners Wall (winners only)Your name, your city, the prize you won and your proof photo or video — the proof is shown only after our team approves it.
Your email, phone number and address are never shown to other users.

If you do not want your proof photo or video on the Winners Wall, simply do not upload one. To have an approved proof removed, email us.

5. Who we share it with

We never sell your personal information. We only share it with these service providers, who help us run the app:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC) — login, database, notifications and app security checks. Firebase privacy
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC) — rewarded video ads. See Advertising.
  • Cloudinary — stores winner proof photos/videos and prize pictures. Cloudinary privacy
  • Courier or delivery service — only for winners: your name, phone number and address, to deliver your prize.
  • Payment service (JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank) — only if a prize is paid in cash.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our users and the app from fraud.

Your data may be stored on servers outside Pakistan that are run by these providers.

6. Advertising

Inaam Bazar shows optional rewarded video ads using Google AdMob. Watching an ad is never required. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and other device information to show and measure ads. You can learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account: kept until you delete it.
  • Records needed for fairness and prizes — draw entries, ticket history, prize claims, winner records and approved winner proofs — are kept even after an account is deleted, so that past draws stay correct and can be checked, and because we may need them for legal reasons or disputes.
  • Coded device ID used for referral checks is kept to stop the same phone from cheating the referral system again.

See Delete Account for the full list of what is deleted and what is kept.

8. Your choices and deletion

  • Delete your account: in the app go to Profile → Account Delete, or email us. Full steps: Delete Account page.
  • See or correct your data: email us and we will help.
  • Notifications: turn them off any time in your phone's settings.
  • Winner proof: uploading it is your choice. Ask us any time to remove it.

9. Security

Your data is stored on Google Firebase with secure (encrypted) connections. Private information such as your email and push token is kept separate from your public profile, and our database rules allow only you and our admin team to read it. No system is 100% safe, but we work hard to protect your information.

10. Age limit

Inaam Bazar is only for people 18 years or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child is using the app, email us and we will delete the account.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the change is important, we will also tell you in the app.
