1. About these rules

Inaam Bazar is a free promotional giveaway and rewards app run by the Inaam Bazar team in Pakistan (the "Organizer"). By using the app or entering any draw, you agree to these Terms and Official Rules and to our Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

Each draw may also show its own details in the app (prize, ticket cost, closing date). Those details are part of these rules.

2. Free to enter — no purchase necessary

No purchase or payment of any kind is ever needed to use Inaam Bazar, earn tickets, enter a draw or win a prize. Paying money will not improve your chance of winning.

The app shows optional rewarded ads to keep it free. Watching ads is never required to take part.

3. Who can take part

  • You must be 18 years or older.
  • You must live in Pakistan. Prizes are delivered only inside Pakistan.
  • One person = one account. Using several accounts, fake accounts or someone else's account is not allowed.
  • Members of the Inaam Bazar team cannot win prizes.

We may ask a winner to show proof of age and identity (for example a CNIC) before giving the prize.

4. Tickets

Tickets are points used inside the app to enter draws. You can earn them for free by, for example:

  • the daily login bonus and streaks,
  • playing the mini-games (Plinko, Lucky Spin, Scratch Card, T-Bird, Quiz),
  • daily missions and badges,
  • inviting friends (referrals),
  • optional rewarded ads (a limited number per day).
Tickets cannot be bought, sold, traded, transferred or exchanged for cash. They have no money value. Daily limits apply to some ways of earning tickets. We may correct a ticket balance that is wrong because of a bug or cheating.

5. How draws work

  • Each draw shows its prize, how many tickets each entry costs, and when it closes.
  • You can enter the same draw up to 4 times. Each extra entry costs more tickets, as shown in the app.
  • Some rewards (such as a free-entry credit) let you enter without spending tickets.
  • Entries close when the draw closes. Tickets spent on an entry are not returned.

6. Odds of winning

Every valid entry has exactly the same chance. Your chance depends on how many entries you have and how many entries there are in total:

Your chance = your entries ÷ total entries in that draw.
Example: if a draw has 500 entries and you have 2, your chance is 2 in 500 (0.4%).

The number of entries in each draw is shown in the app. Each draw has one winner unless the app says otherwise.

7. How the winner is picked

After a draw closes, the winner is picked from all valid entries using a secure computer random number generator, so every entry has the same chance. Every pick is saved in a record that cannot be edited or deleted.

The result is shown to everyone in the app's Live Draw, and the winner is then listed on the Winners Wall.

8. Claiming a prize

  1. The winner is told inside the app and by a notification.
  2. The winner must fill in the prize claim form in the app within 2 days (48 hours) of being picked. The form asks for full name, phone number, delivery address, city and province.
  3. Our team checks the claim and may call the winner or ask for proof of identity.
  4. Once the claim is verified, the prize is sent. The winner can follow the status in the app.

9. When a new winner is picked

The prize may be given to a new, randomly picked entry ("re-draw") if the first winner:

  • does not submit the claim form within 2 days,
  • cannot be contacted, or gives wrong or false details,
  • does not meet the rules (for example under 18, outside Pakistan, or more than one account), or
  • has cheated or broken these rules.

Every re-draw is done with the same fair random method, and the reason is written in our permanent record.

10. Prizes and delivery

  • The prize is the item shown in the draw. Pictures are for illustration; the exact model or colour may differ slightly.
  • No cash alternative: prizes are only given as the item itself. Prizes are never paid as cash, mobile balance, gift cards or any other form of money.
  • If a prize cannot be given, we may give a different item of similar or higher value.
  • Delivery inside Pakistan is free. Prizes cannot be transferred to another person.
  • If any tax applies to a prize under Pakistani law, we will tell the winner before delivery.
  • Warranty, if any, is from the product maker, not from Inaam Bazar.

11. Winners Wall

To show that our giveaways are real, the winner's name, city and prize are shown publicly on the in-app Winners Wall. Winners may also upload a photo or video with their prize; after our team approves it, it is shown on the Winners Wall too. Uploading proof is optional. The winner's phone number and address are never shown.

12. Fair play and bans

We may remove tickets, cancel entries or wins, or ban an account (temporarily or permanently) if someone:

  • uses more than one account, fake accounts or fake referrals,
  • uses hacks, bots, modified apps, emulators or any trick to get tickets or entries,
  • abuses bugs instead of reporting them, or
  • gives false information or harms other users.

If you think your account was banned by mistake, email us.

13. Changes and cancellation

We may change these rules, a draw's details, or the app at any time. Important changes will be shown in the app or on this page. If a draw cannot run fairly (for example because of a technical problem or cheating), we may cancel, pause or re-run it.

14. Limits of responsibility

The app is provided "as is". We are not responsible for lost entries or tickets caused by internet problems, phone problems or events outside our control. As far as the law allows, our total responsibility to you is limited to the value of any prize you have won. These rules are governed by the laws of Pakistan.

15. Google is not a sponsor

Google is not a sponsor of, and is not involved in any way with, Inaam Bazar's giveaways or prizes. Google Play and Google are not responsible for any draw or prize. The same is true for any other app store.
